**Program:** create a semi-formal dining establishment featuring a street-facing bar, equipped with a mirror and a backdrop to showcase the owner's art collection.

The design gives the bar presence from the street, draws guests in, and lets the room work as a gallery for the owner's art without competing with it.

**Scope:** programming, layout and drawings, materials and finishes, lighting, and coordination through build-out.
