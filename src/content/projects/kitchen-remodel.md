The kitchen is the most-used room in the house, and it deserves the biggest investment. This remodel focused on making that investment worth it: a layout that supports how the household actually cooks and gathers, finishes chosen to age well, and lighting layered for both work and evening.

**Scope:** space planning, cabinetry and finish selections, fixtures, lighting, and coordination with the contractor.
